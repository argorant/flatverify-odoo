Bulk

Your whole database as one list

Pick contacts in the list view, open Actions and choose Verify emails with Flatverify. The selection goes out as a single bulk job. A scheduled action follows the job and writes Status, Reason and Checked back to each record as soon as it is done. Re-run it every month: unlimited means a full re-check costs the same as no re-check.

Setup

Connected in a minute

  1. Create a free account at app.flatverify.com (100 free checks, no card).
  2. Copy a key from Settings, API keys.
  3. In Odoo open Settings, Flatverify, paste the key and click Test connection. You see your plan and its speed per minute.
  4. Optional: turn on automatic checks for new and changed emails.
